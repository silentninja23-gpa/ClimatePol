# About ClimatePol

**ClimatePol** is an informational website dedicated to exploring the relationship between climate change and politics. The website features a collection of articles examining how political parties, government policies, public opinion, and international agreements influence climate change and the actions taken to address it. Visitors can explore different perspectives, learn about the politicization of climate science, and understand how political debates shape environmental decisions.

In addition to articles, ClimatePol includes an interactive map highlighting climate-related trends, policies, and impacts across different regions. The website also provides data, statistics, and visualizations that help visitors understand climate change, compare political approaches, and identify patterns in public opinion and government action. By bringing together reliable information, geographic insights, and data-driven analysis, ClimatePol aims to educate visitors, encourage critical thinking, and promote informed discussions about the intersection of climate change and politics.
